**Neon Harbor: Sunfall City** — an original top-down 2D open-world crime game that runs entirely from static files in a desktop browser, with no build step, no assets and no network requests. Explore a procedural sun-soaked coastal city (six named districts, parks and a beach waterfront, a full day cycle) on foot and by car. Steal any car with E and drive it with arcade physics, handbrake drifts, damage and tire smoke, while AI traffic follows the lanes and pedestrians stroll the sidewalks and flee danger. Crimes — theft, collisions, assault, shooting — raise a 0–5 star wanted level; Sunfall PD cruisers spawn, pursue and ram, and the heat only cools when you stay out of sight. Fight on foot with mouse-aim shooting, watch for the WASTED screen, and respawn at Sunfall General. Follow fixer Mara Venn's four-mission story for courier Ria Vale — a package run, a checkpoint chase under police pressure, an escape-and-lay-low job at a beach hideout, and a timed three-bag final score — with on-screen objectives, gold map markers, dialogue banners and cash rewards. The HUD shows health, cash, wanted stars and the current objective beside a live minimap with player, police, traffic and mission blips. All sound is synthesized WebAudio (engine, sirens, gunshots, pickups, UI), and progress — cash plus story position — saves to localStorage so you can continue from the title screen. Controls: WASD/arrows to walk and drive, E to steal or exit a car, mouse to aim and click to shoot, Shift to run, Space handbrake, P pause, M mute.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080 (serve.ts serves game/).
